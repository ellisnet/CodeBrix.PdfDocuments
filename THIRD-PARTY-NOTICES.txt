   native library using CppSharp-generated bindings. The CodeBrix.PdfRasterizer
   library derives its PDFium P/Invoke bindings, rendering approach, and
   several supporting structures from Docnet.Core, simplified into direct
   hand-written P/Invoke declarations.

   The following CodeBrix.PdfRasterizer source components are derived from
   Docnet.Core:

     PdfiumBindings.cs    -  P/Invoke declarations for PDFium functions
                             (document, page, bitmap, rendering, form fill)
     FsMatrix struct      -  2D affine transformation matrix (from FS_MATRIX_)
     FsRectF struct       -  Floating-point clipping rectangle (from FS_RECTF_)
     FpdfFormFillInfo      -  Form fill info structure (from FPDF_FORMFILLINFO)
